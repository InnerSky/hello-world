# Safety and care

Inner Sky is a reflective practice tool. It is not therapy, diagnosis or crisis care, and it never
pretends to be. Care is built into every phase.

## During Attune: notice red flags

If the person mentions any of the following, **don't build an exploratory experience**:
- thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or of harming someone else
- being in danger right now (violence, abuse in progress)
- signs of a medical emergency, intoxication, or severe disorientation
- very recent acute trauma (hours to days) or overwhelming flashbacks
- signs of psychosis or mania (losing touch with what's real, not sleeping for days and feeling invincible)

Instead: respond directly and warmly as Claude, in the conversation. Take it seriously, stay with
them, and encourage reaching a person now: in the US call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis
Lifeline); elsewhere **findahelpline.com** or local emergency services. If they want something to
do in the meantime, offer only a short **stabilization** experience (orienting, extended exhale,
grounding) with no exploration of the difficult material, and keep encouraging human support.

## Trauma-informed defaults (always on)

- **Choice and control**: every step can be skipped; eyes-open options; "if this doesn't suit
  you, breathe normally".
- **Transparency**: the threshold says what's coming and how long it takes.
- **Regulation before depth**: Settle comes before Meet, and resources follow difficulty (pendulation).
- **Titration**: one thing at a time; never ask them to relive a traumatic memory in detail.
- **No forcing**: no instructions to "go deeper into the pain", "stay with it no matter what", or
  "find the gift" in something terrible.
- **Steady button**: always available. It pauses everything and offers grounding and resources.

## Breath and body cautions

- No long breath holds and no hyperventilating breathwork (Wim Hof-style, holotropic) for anyone
  anxious, prone to panic, pregnant, or with heart, blood-pressure, seizure or respiratory conditions.
  When in doubt, use extended exhale or resonance breathing.
- Movement: always offer a seated or minimal option, and "only what feels comfortable".
- Deep relaxation can deepen a shut-down state. For flat or numb people, keep eyes open and keep it short.

## The companion (live Claude in the page)

The engine's built-in instructions already tell the companion never to diagnose, to avoid advice
lists, and to set the exercise aside and encourage contacting a crisis line if anything suggests
danger. Don't override those instructions in a step's `instruction`.

## Privacy

- This repository is **public**. Everything personal (intake notes, readings, scripts, reflections,
  the profile) lives in `private/`, which is git-ignored. Never commit it while the repo is public.
- Experiences are published as **private** Artifacts, visible only to the person unless they share
  them. Reflections save to the artifact's own database, readable only by its owner (and by
  Claude acting for them).
- Steps marked `private: true` never leave the browser, and release steps never store their text.
- Only include in `context` what the companion needs.
