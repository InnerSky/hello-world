# The Compass: reading the moment

Before designing anything, understand where the person actually is. A beautiful experience aimed
at the wrong need doesn't help, so reading the moment matters more than anything after it. This
page says what to listen for and how to turn it into a **Reading**, the brief that every experience
is built from.

## 1. Listen in five layers

| Layer | Listen for | Example signals |
|---|---|---|
| **Body** | energy, tension, arousal, sleep, illness, hunger | "wired", "heavy", "tight chest", "exhausted", "buzzy" |
| **Heart** | emotions and their tone | grief, irritation, longing, numbness, tenderness, joy |
| **Mind** | thoughts, stories, loops, self-talk | "I keep thinking…", "I should…", "what if…" |
| **Life** | what's happening: work, relationships, health, transitions, decisions | a deadline, a breakup, a move, a conflict, a win |
| **Meaning** | direction, values, longing, the bigger question | "what's the point", "I want more…", "who am I now" |

Most people lead with one layer. The need often sits in another. Someone who leads with Mind
("I can't stop planning") may need Body (to come down) before anything else can land.

## 2. Nervous-system state (polyvagal-informed, simplified)

This decides what has to come **first**. You can't think your way out of a dysregulated body, and
insight practices don't land until the system feels safe enough.

- **Activated** (fight/flight): anxious, racing, irritable, restless, overwhelmed, can't sit still.
  → Downshift first: long exhales, orienting, grounding, rhythmic or discharging movement, containment.
  → Avoid at first: long silent sits (they can amplify the noise), deep excavation, breath holds.
- **Shut down** (freeze/collapse): flat, numb, heavy, foggy, hopeless, "can't be bothered".
  → Upshift gently: orienting with the eyes, small movements, humming, warmth, simple choices, a little play.
  → Avoid: deep relaxation or long eyes-closed practice (it can deepen the collapse), heavy material.
- **Mixed** ("tired but wired"): discharge, then settle. Shake → sway → slow exhale.
- **Open** (calm, curious, okay-ish): there's room to go deeper, into inquiry, meaning, creativity or challenge.

When unsure, assume a little activated and open with something regulating. It rarely hurts.

## 3. Stated need, sensed need

- **Stated need**: what they say they need ("I need to focus", "I need to stop feeling guilty").
  Always honor it. It's the doorway, and it's theirs.
- **Sensed need**: what you hear underneath. Use a needs vocabulary (Marshall Rosenberg's NVC is a
  good one): rest, safety, ease, being seen, being understood, connection, belonging, autonomy, choice,
  competence, contribution, meaning, play, beauty, expression, clarity, order, mourning, celebration.
- The experience **addresses the stated need on its surface and serves the sensed need underneath**.
  Offer, never override: "you said focus; part of what I heard was permission to rest first."

## 4. The movement: what kind of shift is needed

Choose **one primary movement** and at most one secondary. This is the most important design choice.

| Movement | When you hear… | Typical lineages |
|---|---|---|
| **Settle** | overwhelm, anxiety, racing, can't land | breath science, Somatic Experiencing, polyvagal, yoga nidra |
| **Feel** | an emotion that wants room: grief, anger, fear, longing | Focusing, RAIN, emotion-focused therapy, Gestalt |
| **Understand** | confusion, a pattern repeating, "why do I keep…" | IFS, coherence therapy, narrative therapy, inquiry |
| **Release** | holding on: resentment, a past event, a should, a role | ritual, ACT defusion, The Work, forgiveness practices |
| **Reclaim** | powerlessness, people-pleasing, lost boundaries, small | Stoic dichotomy of control, IFS Self-leadership, embodiment |
| **Orient** | a decision, too many options, lost direction | values work (ACT), future-self, solution-focused, discernment (Ignatian) |
| **Nourish** | self-criticism, depletion, burnout, shame | self-compassion (Neff, Gilbert), rest, loving-kindness |
| **Enliven** | flatness, boredom, numbness, low energy, stuck | play, movement, awe, novelty, sensory delight |
| **Connect** | loneliness, separateness, cynicism | loving-kindness, gratitude, awe, Joanna Macy, attachment |
| **Savor** | things are good, a win, a milestone, a quiet day | savoring (Bryant), taking in the good (Hanson), celebration |

## 5. Capacity and container

- **Time**: honor it exactly (5, 10, 20, 40 minutes).
- **Depth**: gentle / medium / deep. Ask if unclear, and default to a notch gentler than they ask for.
- **Setting**: can they speak aloud, move, close their eyes? Are they alone? Headphones?
- **Time of day**: morning leans toward Orient and Enliven; evening toward Settle, Release, Savor.
  It also picks the sky. Don't open a dawn sky at midnight.
- **What they already know works** (and what doesn't). Read `private/profile.md`.

## 6. The growing edge

What is the one step of growth this person is ready for **today**? A good experience lands
just at the edge: not so safe that nothing happens, not so far that they brace or defend. Name it
in one sentence ("to let the tiredness be real without making it mean something about them").

## 7. The Reading (write this before composing)

Save it as `private/sessions/<id>/reading.md`:

```markdown
# Reading: <date>, <working title>

## In their words
- "<verbatim phrases worth echoing>"

## Weather
<one paragraph: what it's like in there today, across the five layers>

## State
Nervous system: <activated / shut down / mixed / open>, because <evidence>

## Need
- Stated: …
- Sensed: …

## Movement
Primary: <one> · Secondary: <optional>

## Growing edge
<one sentence>

## Council (5–7 lenses; see lenses.md)
- <Lens>: what it sees · what it offers
- …

## Throughline
<one image or metaphor that holds the whole experience together, ideally drawn from their own words>

## Shape
<arc pattern from arcs.md> · <minutes> · skies: <sequence>
1. Arrive: …
2. Settle: …
3. …

## Measure
<the before/after question, e.g. "How much room is there inside? 0 crammed, 10 spacious">

## Care notes
<anything from safety.md that applies; what to avoid for this person>
```
